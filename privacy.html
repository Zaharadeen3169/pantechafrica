Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what Pan Tech Africa collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Your Pi username and Pi user ID, provided when you sign in with Pi.
  • Delivery details you enter when you order: name, phone number, address, city and country.
  • Your orders, payments and support requests.

What we never collect

We never ask for or store your Pi wallet passphrase or private keys. We do not use email or social logins.

How we use it

We use your information to process and deliver orders, confirm payments, answer support requests and prevent fraud. We share delivery details only with the people who deliver your order. We do not sell your data.

Your choices

You can contact support to ask for a copy of your data or to request deletion, apart from records we must keep for completed orders.

Contact

Use Chat with Support inside the app.
